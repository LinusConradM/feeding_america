What you can explore

Five questions the platform helps you answer, and the method behind each one

Ask in plain English

Type a question and the AI Data Analyst, powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash, writes and runs a Pandas query against the full county dataset, then explains the result. Answers come from the data, not from the model's memory.

Did this policy actually work?

Pick a state and the year a policy took effect. Difference-in-Differences compares its food insecurity trend with a control group, so you can estimate the policy's effect rather than just a correlation.

Which counties cluster together?

K-Means clustering groups counties with similar profiles, using location so clusters stay geographically connected. A Sankey diagram shows which counties moved between groups from one year to another.

What's the trend over time?

Follow food insecurity from 2009 to 2023, compare before and during COVID-19, and see SARIMAX forecasts for the nation or a single state.

Which counties don't fit the pattern?

An Isolation Forest scans every county to flag the ones whose combination of food insecurity, poverty, and other indicators is unusual compared with the rest.
